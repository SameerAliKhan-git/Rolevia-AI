Tsenta surfaces roles that fit your résumé and preferences, including location, salary, experience level, and role family. Matches include an explanation, and you can paste a job URL to add a role to your application workflow.

A successful application can produce a receipt with the submitted fields, open-ended answers, résumé, any cover letter used, and confirmation details. You can review the record and flag anything you want changed for next time.

Tsenta submits through supported employer application systems using the information and materials in your account. Employer systems and hiring teams control their own review processes, so Tsenta does not promise that automated assistance is undetectable.

For each role, Tsenta uses the job description and your saved background to prepare a tailored résumé. You can review and edit it before submission or choose more automated behavior in your settings.

Tsenta can surface known sponsorship signals and use the work-authorization information in your account when answering application questions. Employer policies can change, and Tsenta does not guarantee sponsorship.

Connecting your inbox is optional and is never required to use Tsenta. It does two things. Tsenta reads the job-related mail your applications generate, such as confirmations, recruiter replies, interview invitations, and rejections, so your tracker updates itself instead of you filing every message by hand, and so verification codes some employers email mid-application can be entered for you. It also drafts replies to recruiters and saves them to your own drafts folder, where you read, edit, and send them yourself. Tsenta does not send mail from your account on its own. Tsenta only looks for messages related to your job search, never uses your mail for advertising, and never uses it to train AI models. You can disconnect at any time from settings, which revokes access immediately.
