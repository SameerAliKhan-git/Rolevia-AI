## 2. Key Competitive Advantages of My Platform

1. **Global & Dual-Currency Localization**: Tsenta is heavily USD-centric. Our platform natively supports **₹ INR, $ USD, and € EUR** with localized Razorpay integration (Cards, UPI, Netbanking), opening immediate access to the enormous Indian tech applicant pool and EMEA candidates.
2. **Superior Claymorphic UI Craft**: Tsenta's dashboard is a standard corporate flat layout. Our Claymorphic design creates an engaging, high-end consumer software experience that users enjoy interacting with.
3. **Rigorous Live Link Health Auto-Purge**: Our platform implements active HTTP 200 validation that automatically purges taken-down employer job links, whereas Tsenta displays legacy broken links and negative timestamp bugs.
4. **Transparency & Truth-Grounded Tailoring**: Our architecture enforces that resume optimization never fabricates employment history or false metrics, strictly differentiating ATS keyword formatting from factual qualifications.
